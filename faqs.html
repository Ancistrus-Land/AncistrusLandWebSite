100% Live Arrival Guarantee (LAG) Across Canada

Every fish and shrimp order is shipped in custom insulated Styrofoam boxes with medical-grade pure oxygen and climate-controlled heat packs (winter) or ice packs (summer). We guarantee 100% live arrival across all Canadian provinces.

Canadian winters require extreme care. We use thick 1.5-inch molded EPS Styrofoam shipping boxes, premium 72-hour Uniheat packs to keep water temperatures between 22°C–26°C, and double-layered bags charged with medical oxygen. Shipments are handled via express courier (Canada Post Priority, Purolator, or FedEx Overnight).

In the rare event of a DOA, please take a clear, unopened photo or video of the bag within 2 hours of parcel delivery and send it to our WhatsApp or email (+1 613 266 5285 / sudarsha.wijenayake@gmail.com). We will issue an immediate replacement or full refund.

To protect livestock from being held over in transit facilities over the weekend, live shipments are dispatched on Mondays and Tuesdays. Ottawa/Gatineau area orders can also be scheduled for local hand-off or courier pickup.
