100% Live Arrival Guarantee (LAG)

Every fish and shrimp order is shipped in custom insulated Styrofoam boxes with medical-grade pure oxygen. We guarantee live arrival to any address across Sri Lanka.

All livestock is fasted for 24-48 hours prior to shipping to keep the transport water pristine. Specimens are double-bagged with pure oxygen, temperature-stabilized using thermal insulation, and dispatched via priority point-to-point courier or express train cargo.

In the rare event of a DOA, please take a clear, unopened photo or video of the bag within 2 hours of delivery and send it to our WhatsApp (+1 613 266 5285). We will either issue a free replacement or store credit immediately.

To protect our pets from weekend parcel delays, live animal shipments are dispatched exclusively on Mondays, Tuesdays, and Wednesdays. Dry goods can be shipped any day.
