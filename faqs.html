Specialized Thermal Packing Across Canada

Every fish and shrimp order is packed in insulated boxes with pure oxygen and climate-adjusted seasonal heat packs (winter) or cooling packs (summer) for dependable Canadian transit.

Canadian winters require extreme care. We use thick 1.5-inch molded EPS Styrofoam shipping boxes, premium 72-hour Uniheat packs to keep water temperatures between 22°C–26°C, and double-layered bags charged with medical oxygen. Shipments are handled via express courier (Canada Post Priority, Purolator, or FedEx Overnight).

In the rare event of a transit issue, please take a clear, unopened photo or video of the bag and send it to our WhatsApp or email (+1 613 857-7381 / contact@ancistrusland.com). We will assist you promptly.

To protect livestock from being held over in transit facilities over the weekend, live shipments are dispatched on Mondays and Tuesdays. Ottawa/Gatineau area orders can also be scheduled for local hand-off or courier pickup.

We accept Interac e-Transfer (direct from any Canadian bank or credit union), major credit cards (Visa/Mastercard via secure payment invoice), and Cash on Delivery for Ottawa/Gatineau local handoffs.
