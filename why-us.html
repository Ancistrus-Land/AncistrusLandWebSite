The Ancistrus Standard

Dedicated to Healthy, Acclimated Aquatic Life in Canada

At Ancistrus Land, we are passionate hobbyists and breeders dedicated to bringing the healthiest, disease-free aquatic livestock directly to Canadian fishkeepers. We founded our breeding operations in Ottawa to eliminate the risks of unconditioned imports and cold-weather transit casualties.

Every fish, shrimp, and snail in our care undergoes strict 14-day quarantine, preventive conditioning, and is packed with winter heat packs and medical pure oxygen for transit across all Canadian provinces.
