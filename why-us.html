The Ancistrus Standard

Dedicated to Healthy, Acclimated Aquatic Life in Canada

At Ancistrus Land, we are passionate hobbyists and breeders dedicated to bringing vibrant, acclimated aquatic livestock directly to Canadian fishkeepers. We founded our breeding operations in Ottawa to focus on captive-bred quality and careful conditioning.

Every fish, shrimp, and snail in our care receives attentive daily monitoring, nutritious conditioning, and is prepared with protective insulated courier packaging for secure transit across Canadian provinces.
