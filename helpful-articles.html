The Drip Acclimation Method for Shrimp & Discus

Sudden shifts in TDS, pH, and temperature are the #1 cause of livestock stress. Learn our foolproof drip method to introduce your new pets safely.

Key Steps:
  • Float sealed bag 15 mins for temperature balance.
  • Empty fish into clean bucket with shipping water.
  • Use airline tubing with valve to drip 2-3 drops/sec for 45 mins.
  • Net the fish into aquarium without adding shipping water.
Water Chemistry

Canadian Municipal Water & Aquariums

How to handle Canadian tap water (chloramines, GH/KH levels, and seasonal variations) to keep delicate breeds like Discus and Caridina flourishing.

Key Recommendations:
  • Use a high-potency water conditioner (e.g. Seachem Prime) for chloramines.
  • Discus thrive at 28°C - 30°C (82°F - 86°F); use a reliable digital heater.
  • Perform regular 25% weekly water changes.
  • Maintain zero ammonia and nitrites.

Feeding for Vibrant Colors & Rapid Growth

From spirulina wafers and natural driftwood grazing for Ancistrus plecos to beef heart pastes and astaxanthin-rich granules for Red Melon and Blue Diamond Discus.

Feeding Tips:
  • Feed only what they consume in 2 minutes.
  • Provide high-fiber vegetable supplements for bottom dwellers.
  • Incorporate live or frozen bloodworms twice a week.
