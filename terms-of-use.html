2. Canada-Wide Live Arrival Guarantee (LAG) Policy

We take exceptional care in packing your live aquatic animals with pure oxygen, thermal wraps, 72-hour heat packs (in sub-zero temperatures), and insulated boxing. Our Live Arrival Guarantee is valid across Canada under the following terms:

  • You must receive the package upon the first delivery attempt by the courier or pick it up from the depot within 2 hours of arrival.
  • In the rare event of a casualty, provide clear, unopened photos or videos of the livestock in the original shipping bag within 2 hours of delivery.
  • Upon confirmation, we will provide an immediate replacement or full refund.
